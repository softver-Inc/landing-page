Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

Softver SRL ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MACAJAY mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

MACAJAY is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • Player Names: Names entered during gameplay are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Game Preferences: Your language preference and settings are stored locally on your device.

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (email, phone number, address)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Payment or financial information

2. How We Use Your Information

The limited data stored on your device is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain gameplay functionality.
  • Remember your language and settings preferences.

3. Data Storage

All data generated through MACAJAY is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: Subject to their respective privacy policies when you download or update the App.
  • Analytics (if applicable): We may use anonymous, aggregated analytics to understand general usage patterns and improve the App. No personally identifiable information is collected through these services.

5. Children's Privacy

MACAJAY is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 17. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, biometric authentication, encryption). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
